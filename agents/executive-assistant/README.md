# Professional Executive Assistant

**Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot prompt (paste into Copilot Chat)

Turns a rough, unpolished list of what you did this week into a clear, confident, meeting-ready update for a weekly 1:1 with your manager.

## What it does

Acting as a professional executive assistant, it:

- Rewrites rough notes into clear, concise updates
- Highlights key achievements and the outcomes/benefits they produced
- Notes concerns, risks, or obstacles where relevant
- Makes each item **SMART** (specific, outcome-focused, time-bound where possible)
- Emphasizes the impact of your work on the team, delivery, or objectives
- Keeps the tone professional and factual (not inflated)
- Flags each item as **Complete**, **In Progress**, or **Requires Manager Input**

## How to use it

1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (Copilot Chat in Teams or [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)) with a Copilot-licensed account.
2. Paste the prompt from [`prompt.txt`](./prompt.txt).
3. Add your rough list of the week's tasks and let Copilot structure the output.

## Prerequisites

- Microsoft 365 Copilot
