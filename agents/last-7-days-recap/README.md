# Last 7 Days Recap (Manager Update)

**Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot prompt (paste into Copilot Chat)

Generates a concise weekly status update by scanning your last 7 days of emails, meetings, and Teams chats — so you can quickly share progress with your manager.

## What it does

Drafts a structured weekly update covering:

- **Key wins** from the past week
- **Decisions made**
- **Blockers** that need your manager's input
- **What you're focused on** next week

## How to use it

1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (Copilot Chat in Teams or [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)) with a Copilot-licensed account.
2. Paste the prompt from [`prompt.txt`](./prompt.txt).
3. Ask Copilot to refine the tone and level of detail for your manager.

**Take it further:** ask for two variants — a short executive summary and a detailed version with action items.

## Prerequisites

- Microsoft 365 Copilot
- Access to Outlook, Teams, and your calendar
