# Wrap Up Your Day & Plan Tomorrow

**Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot prompt (paste into Copilot Chat)

A daily wrap-up and next-day planning prompt that turns a chaotic meeting day into clear, high-impact follow-through. It summarizes what you accomplished today, pulls tasks out of your meetings and email, then organizes tomorrow into a categorized meeting roster with prep notes and a balanced break plan.

## What it does

Produces a three-part briefing:

1. **Today** — a short paragraph on today's work, a list of tasks that came out of your meetings, and the important items flagged from your email.
2. **Your Meetings Tomorrow** — a table of tomorrow's meetings (organizer, details, category: personal/internal/external, and what to know), plus a breakdown of what % of your time goes to each category.
3. **Preparation for Tomorrow** — a concise brief on what matters most for a productive day, with suggested break timing.

## How to use it

1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (Copilot Chat in Teams or [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)) with a Copilot-licensed account.
2. Paste the prompt from [`prompt.txt`](./prompt.txt).
3. Edit the "Preparation for Tomorrow" section with your specific goals (e.g., a customer call or a project deliverable).
4. Save the output to OneNote, Teams, or Planner as a daily ritual.

**Take it further:** follow up with — *"Can you also show me the time spent on different categories as a pie chart in Mermaid JS."*

## Prerequisites

- Microsoft 365 Copilot
- Access to Outlook, Teams, and Planner/To Do data
