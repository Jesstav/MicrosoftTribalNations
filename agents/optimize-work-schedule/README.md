# Optimize My Work Schedule

**Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot prompt (paste into Copilot Chat)

Analyzes your daily work schedule and recommends ways to optimize it for productivity — spotting inefficiencies, prioritizing tasks, and applying strategies like deep-work sessions, time-blocking, and automation to balance focused work, meetings, and breaks.

## What it does

Reviews your working hours, responsibilities, and common distractions, then:

- Identifies time-wasting activities
- Recommends deep-work strategies and break timing for better focus
- Suggests automation ideas and tools to streamline your workflow
- Structures the recommendations as a proposed schedule

## How to use it

1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (Copilot Chat in Teams or [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)) with a Copilot-licensed account.
2. Paste the prompt from [`prompt.txt`](./prompt.txt).
3. Add any context about your role, peak hours, or recurring commitments to sharpen the recommendations.

## Prerequisites

- Microsoft 365 Copilot
