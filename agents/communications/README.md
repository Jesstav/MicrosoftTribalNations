# Communications Assistant

A Microsoft 365 Copilot agent that helps Tribal Nation government, program, and enterprise staff write clear, respectful, and consistent communications — emails, letters, announcements, newsletters, social posts, and press releases — matched to the audience and channel. It always drafts for review, never invents facts or quotes, and never sends on your behalf.

> **Custom agent:** Built for this gallery (not adapted from a community sample). Tailor the "House Style" section to your Nation's style guide, official names, and approval workflow.

## Why it fits Tribal Nations

Supports **member/citizen notices, employee comms, partner and media relations, and public announcements** with culturally respectful language and consistent voice. Includes optional guidance to route Indigenous-language content to a qualified language keeper for review.

## What it does

- **Intake** — Confirms purpose, audience, channel, tone, length, deadline, and must-include facts before drafting.
- **Draft** — Produces channel-appropriate drafts (email, letter, newsletter, social, press release) and marks missing specifics as `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
- **Refine** — Offers shorter/warmer/more-formal or plain-language versions and explains its edits.
- **Translate/adapt (optional)** — Adapts reading level or language, always recommending human review.
- **Package** — Assembles a final draft, summary, headline options, and a list of remaining placeholders.

## How to build this agent

This is a **Microsoft 365 Copilot declarative agent** — built in **Copilot Agent Builder**.

1. Open **[copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com)** with a **Microsoft 365 Copilot**–licensed account.
2. Go to **Agents → Create agent → Configure**.
3. **Name:** `Communications Assistant`
4. **Description:** paste the contents of [`description.txt`](./description.txt).
5. **Instructions:** paste the contents of [`instructions.txt`](./instructions.txt).
6. Add your style guide, official terminology, and boilerplate as knowledge, then create and test.
